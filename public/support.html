How We Calculate Your Daily Needs

We use the Mifflin–St Jeor equation to estimate your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR):

BMR = 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age (years) + 5

Your total daily calories are then calculated by applying your activity level:

Calories = BMR × Activity Factor

We make a small adjustment based on your Body Mass Index (BMI = weight / (height/100)²):

  • If BMI ≥ 25 (overweight), we subtract 500 kcal to promote a gradual loss
  • If BMI < 18.5 (underweight), we add 500 kcal to support healthy gain

Finally, your macronutrient targets are set as a percentage of total calories:

  • Protein: 25% → grams = (0.25 × Calories) / 4
  • Fat: 25% → grams = (0.25 × Calories) / 9
  • Carbs: 50% → grams = (0.50 × Calories) / 4
